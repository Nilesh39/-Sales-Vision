Key Diagnostic: The 50% Discount Trap (-$37,830 Leak)

Our data analysis proved that products sold at 0% discount deliver a healthy 43.56% margin. However, deep discounting at 50% resulted in an outright loss of -13.20% across 773 orders. Restricting sales rep discounts to a maximum of 25% will immediately reclaim $55,000+ in annual profit.

1. Laptop Margin Bundling

Laptops account for $1.32M (29%) of sales but carry only 18% margins due to high manufacturing costs. Mandatory bundling with high-margin webcams and headsets (45%+ margin) raises basket profitability.

2. East Coast Growth Strategy

The East territory accounts for under 20% of sales despite holding major metros (NYC, Boston, Philadelphia). Reallocating 15% more marketing budget captures untapped market share without hurting profitability.

3. Seasonal Supply Staging

Demand sharply surges in July and December. Procuring component inventory in May and October eliminates backorders and lowers average shipping turnaround below 3.0 days.
